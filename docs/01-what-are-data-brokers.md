# What Are Data Brokers? (and why they have your info)

*Part of the Data Broker Removal Walkthrough Guide — Copyright (c) 2026 Asdale Dynamics, LLC. Education only; this is not legal advice.*

## The short version

A **data broker** is a company that collects personal information about you from public
records and bought sources, then packages it to sell or display — people-search sites,
background-check services, and marketing data companies. They didn't collect it because
you signed up with them. You visited a courthouse record feed, a marketing list, or a
public directory, and they bought a copy.

Typical things brokers hold:

- Name, age, addresses (current + past), phone numbers, email addresses
- Household members, relatives, "associated people"
- Partial (and sometimes guessed) SSN digits, birth month/day
- Property records, business registrations, licenses, court filings
- Marketing profiles: estimated income, interests, "likely to move"

## The main kinds of broker

| Kind | Examples | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| People-search / background check | Whitepages, Spokeo, MyLife, Intelius, BeenVerified, TruthFinder, FastPeopleSearch, Radaris, Instant Checkmate | Sell individual "reports" about you to anyone |
| Marketing / analytics data companies | Acxiom, Experian Marketing Services, Epsilon | Build profiles used for ad targeting and list sales |
| Risk & identity companies | LexisNexis, Thomson Reuters CLEAR, Experian/Lexis "identity" data | Feed banks, landlords, insurers, employers |
| Public-record aggregators | Tracking sites, property and license lookups | Scrape public databases into searchable profiles |

## How your data got there in the first place

- **Public records** — property deeds, voter rolls (in most states), court records,
  professional licenses (like the nursing registry), business filings. Indiana's
  public records are widely republished.
- **Data you typed into forms** — contests, store loyalty cards, warranty cards,
  "sign up to save 10%."
- **Purchased lists** — your name sold on and on between list brokers.
- **Scrapers** — anything an "old username" once revealed, some sites have rehashed
  and republished.

## Why removal "sticks" only partially

An honest expectation, stated plainly:

1. **Deletion is per-broker.** Removing yourself from Spokeo does nothing to
   BeenVerified. They buy from overlapping sources but are separate companies.
2. **They may re-acquire you later.** A new public record (address change, license
   renewal, small-claims filing) can re-add you. Filters and machine matching are
   imperfect, and that's why renewals matter (see `06-renewals-calendar.md`).
3. **The first round is the hard one.** 20–30 minutes per broker, times 10–15 brokers,
   once. After that, maintenance is a few hours a year.

## Why bother at all?

- Less exposure of your home address linked to your profession and family.
- Fewer results when someone googles you — a lot of "page one of results" noise
  comes from exactly these sites.
- Lower risk from the imposter/scam circle that scrapes these pages
  (relatives can be contacted by people quoting "details" from a broker page).
- Keeps a clearer separation between your nursing career, your writing, and your home.
