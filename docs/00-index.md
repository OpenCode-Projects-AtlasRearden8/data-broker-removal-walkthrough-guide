## Who this is for

Anyone who's ever searched their own name and found a people-search site listing
their home address, relatives, and phone number — and wants it gone without
paying a subscription company to do it. Written in plain English, step by step,
for beginners. If you can write an email, you can do this.

## The interactive checklist

Open `site/index.html` in any browser (or serve it — see repo root README).
It's a static, offline-first page with the whole plan as a tickable checklist,
progress tracking saved locally in your browser, filterable sections, and a
print-friendly layout. **No data leaves your device.**

## The honest expectations, up front

- Removing yourself is per-broker, takes one focused
  afternoon per ~10 sites, then ~60 minutes a year forever.
- Public records (property, licenses, court) legitimately exist. The goal is
  less noise and exposure, not invisibility.
- No scraping or automated hammering of any site. Normal human visits only.

## Safety notes

- Never paste a full SSN, full ID, or anyone else's personal data into requests.
- If a listing puts you in physical danger (stalking/DV), prioritize the major
  people-search removals and Google's "Results about you" removal same day, and
  seek specialist help (see doc 07).
