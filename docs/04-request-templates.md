# Free Manual Request Templates

*Copy, fill in the [brackets], send. No personal data of anyone else's should ever
be pasted into these — only your own. Keep the notes about which law you cite;
still education-only, not legal advice.*

## Which template to use

| Situation | Template |
|---|---|
| Most brokers (email/privacy desk) | #1 Generic deletion + opt-out |
| You live in a state with a privacy law (`02-state-removal-rights.md`) | #2 State-law deletion |
| Site says "it's public record, sorry" | #3 Firm follow-up |
| Postal mail (old-school forms demand a letter) | #4 Postal letter |
| Broker never responded at all | #5 Follow-up / polite nudge |
| Escalation | #6 Complaint to AG / FTC |

Send from your dedicated project email. Attach nothing except, when a form *explicitly*
requires it, the profile URL pasted **in the body text** — never a screenshot with
other people's information on it.

## Template 1 — Generic deletion + opt-out email

**To:** the broker's privacy/opt-out address (verify it on their own site)

**Subject:** Removal of my personal information — [Your Full Name]

```
Hello,

I am requesting the removal of my personal information from your database and
from any consumer-facing profile pages you operate.

Details of my listing:
- Name: [Your Full Name] (also appears as [prior/variant name, if any])
- Listing URL: [paste the profile page URL]
- City / state of the listing: [City, ST]

Please:
1. Delete or suppress my profile page so it no longer appears publicly, and
2. Remove my personal information from your searchable or sellable databases
   and from any lists you sell, share, or license to third parties.
3. Remove me from any "sale," "sharing," or targeted-advertising use of my
   personal information as those terms are commonly used under state privacy
   laws.

Please confirm in writing once the removal is complete. If you need additional
verification, I am happy to provide my full name (above) and mailing city —
please do not require a government ID number or full SSN for a standard removal.

Thank you,
[Your Full Name]
[Your project email address]
[Date]
```

## Template 2 — State privacy-law deletion (use if your state is listed in doc 02)

**Subject:** Deletion request under [State]'s privacy law — [Your Full Name]

```
Hello,

I am a resident of [State] and this is a formal request under [State]'s
consumer data privacy law (e.g., California CCPA/CPRA; Colorado CPA; Texas
TDPSA; Indiana's privacy law effective 2026 — name only the one that applies
to you):

1. DELETE all personal information you hold about me, including derived
   profiles, from your systems and from any third parties to whom you have
   sold, shared, or licensed it;
2. OPT ME OUT of the sale, sharing, and targeted advertising use of my
   personal information, now and going forward;
3. Confirm in writing which categories of my data you process and how you
   fulfilled this request.

Identifying information: [Full name], [City, ST], listing URL [if applicable,
paste URL]. I verify my identity as: name above + city/state
and the URL of the listing I am referencing. If your verification policy
requires more, the standard verification practice for removal requests (my
name and city) should be sufficient — please use the least-intrusive method
available.

Please respond within the time period your law requires (commonly 30–45 days).

Sincerely,
[Your Full Name]
[Project email], [Date]
```

*(Fill in the single law that applies to you — don't list all of them.)*

## Template 3 — Firm follow-up after "it's public record"

**Subject:** Re: Removal request — requesting suppression of my profile (Ticket #___)

```
Hello,

Thank you for your reply. I understand my source information may originate in
public records. My request is about your republishing of it, not the underlying
record.

I am asking you — as your privacy policy and standard industry practice for
people-search sites allow — to SUPPRESS the public display of my profile:

1. Remove or no-index the public profile page at [URL] so it no longer
   appears in search engines or on your site;
2. Remove my name from your searchable index as it relates to [City, ST];
3. Do not display my information to third parties or in lists you license.

Many comparable services suppress listings on request via their own
opt-out/suppression forms; I am asking that you apply the same policy here.

Please confirm when complete.

[Your Full Name]
[Date]
```

## Template 4 — Postal letter (when a form demands mail)

```
[Date]

[Company Privacy Office — full mailing address from their privacy policy]

Re: Removal of personal information — [Your Full Name]

Dear Privacy Officer,

I request that you remove my personal information from your databases and
public profile pages, including the listing at [URL], associated with
[Name] in [City, ST]. Please also opt me out of the sale, sharing, and
licensing of my information.

[If applicable — a resident of [State] invoking their state privacy law,
add one sentence citing it and the deletion right.]

Please send written confirmation when complete. Replies to:
[Project email]     [Mailing address or P.O. box, if you prefer postal only]

Sincerely,
[Signature]
[Your Full Name]
```

*Postal-only tip: use a P.O. box or a work address if you don't want your home
address on yet another company's list. Many brokers accept email copies first —
check the privacy policy before printing.*

## Template 5 — The polite nudge (no response in 2–3 weeks)

**Subject:** Follow-up: removal request sent [date] — [Your Full Name]

```
Hello,

On [date] I requested removal of my personal information (name: [Your Full
Name], listing: [URL]). I haven't received confirmation yet.

Could you confirm the request's status and, if it's queued, the expected
timeline? If the original request didn't reach the right team, please route
this message as a fresh removal request.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

## Template 6 — Escalation right (only after 2 polite tries)

- **State Attorney General consumer complaint** — search "[Your State] attorney
  general consumer complaint" and use their official form. Mention: you invoked
  a state privacy right (if applicable), you have 2 dated requests, and no
  confirmation.
- **FTC** — reportfraud.ftc.gov (limited enforcement against privacy practices
  here, but AN AG will care, and the FTC does track broker complaints).
- **Permission Slip (free, Consumer Reports)** — can submit deletion requests on
  your behalf to participating companies; a good wedge when a broker ignores
  individuals.

## After you send: the checklist habits that make it stick

- [ ] Confirmation link clicked (web-form flows)
- [ ] Ticket/reference number saved in your tracking sheet
- [ ] Confirmation email saved in a "Removals — done" label/folder
- [ ] Follow-up calendar reminder set for +3 weeks, in case of silence
- [ ] Renewal reminder set for +3–4 months (see docs/06-renewals-calendar.md)
